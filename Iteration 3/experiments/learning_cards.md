# MVP Learning Card – Social Proof / Friends Activity

## Hypothesis Tested

Students will feel more confident exploring and choosing clubs if they can see friend activity, peer recommendations, or other forms of social proof on the Profile page.

## What We Built

For Iteration 3, we implemented a Social Proof / Friends Activity feature on the Profile page of the MVP.

This feature includes:

* friend activity related to specific clubs
* short peer-style recommendations
* suggested friends
* Add Friend / Following interaction
* links from friend activity to related club detail pages

This was implemented as a functional React feature using mock data, rather than as a static wireframe.

## Evidence Collected

During general interviews, participants repeatedly said that friend recommendations and student experiences influence their confidence when choosing clubs.

In product testing, participants responded positively to the Social Proof feature. They said that seeing friend activity made the platform feel more realistic and trustworthy. Participants also said that peer recommendations could help them decide which clubs are worth exploring.

However, participants also explained that this feature would become more valuable if it included real student reviews, ratings, or testimonials from current members.

## Learning

The Social Proof feature partially validated our Social Influence hypothesis.

Participants did not see friend activity as the only factor in choosing a club, but they described it as a useful confidence signal. This suggests that social proof works best as a supporting feature that helps reduce decision uncertainty after students have already found clubs they are interested in.

The feature also supports our broader learning from Iteration 2 and Iteration 3: students do not only need more club information. They need information that helps them judge whether a club is active, trustworthy, and worth their time.

## Decision

We will keep the Social Proof / Friends Activity feature as part of the MVP.

For future iterations, we would improve this feature by adding:

* real student reviews
* club ratings
* member testimonials
* “what to expect at your first event” comments
* indicators of how active each club is

This would make the feature stronger and help students understand the human side of clubs, not just the basic club information.

## Status

Partially validated.
