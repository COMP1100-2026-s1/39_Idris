Test Cards 

Test Card 1: Clear club information

Hypothesis

We believe that UQ students are more likely to consider joining a club if the club information is clear, structured, and easy to compare.

Test

We will ask participants to use the MVP and browse the club list. They will be asked to find a club that interests them and explain whether the information shown is enough to help them decide.

Metric

The hypothesis is supported if participants can understand what a club is about and can explain why they would or would not join it without needing extra information from outside the platform.

Evidence

Evidence comes from interview transcripts, MVP testing feedback, and user comments during testing.

Result

Mostly supported. Participants found the structured club cards and club detail information helpful, but some still wanted more practical details such as event frequency, cost, time commitment, and student reviews.

Decision

Keep structured club information as a core MVP feature. In future versions, add more verified and practical club details.

Test Card 2: Search and filtering

Hypothesis

We believe that search and category filtering will help students find suitable clubs faster than browsing a long unstructured list.

Test

Participants will use the search bar and category filters to find clubs related to their interests, such as Sports, Faculty, Culture, or Others.

Metric

The hypothesis is supported if participants can narrow down the club list and find relevant clubs without confusion.

Evidence

Evidence comes from MVP testing observations and participant feedback.

Result

Supported. Search and filtering made the browsing process easier and reduced the amount of scrolling needed.

Decision

Keep search and category filtering as important MVP features. These features directly support the problem of helping students compare and choose clubs more easily.

Test Card 3: Join and profile feature

Hypothesis

We believe that allowing users to join clubs and view joined clubs on their profile will make the MVP feel more like a working product rather than a static prototype.

Test

Participants will select a club, use the join function, and then check the profile page to see whether the joined club information is visible.

Metric

The hypothesis is supported if participants understand that the join action worked and can find their joined club information on the profile page.

Evidence

Evidence comes from MVP testing and the implemented profile page feature.

Result

Partly supported. The join function helps make the MVP feel functional, but the profile page can be improved with clearer personalisation and stronger feedback after joining.

Decision

Keep the join and profile features. Add small profile improvements, such as a Club Preference Summary, to make the profile page more useful.
