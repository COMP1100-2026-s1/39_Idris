# Value Proposition Canvas (Iteration 3)

## Introduction

The Value Proposition Canvas was refined using evidence collected across Iterations 1, 2, and 3. Interviews, competitor analysis, prototype testing, and MVP testing consistently showed that students can discover clubs, but often struggle to confidently decide which club to join.

The purpose of this canvas is to show how the UQ Club Discovery Hub MVP addresses this problem through a functional software solution.

---

## Customer Profile

The primary customer segment is UQ undergraduate and postgraduate students seeking extracurricular involvement and community engagement.

### Customer Jobs

* Discover clubs and societies
* Compare multiple club options
* Understand club activities and expectations
* Meet new people
* Build skills and networks
* Join clubs that fit their interests and schedules

### Pains

* Difficulty confidently choosing between clubs
* Information scattered across multiple platforms
* Vague or outdated club descriptions
* Unclear time commitment and expectations
* Too many options to compare manually
* Fear of joining a club that is not a good fit
* Social anxiety when joining alone

### Gains

* Clear and trustworthy club information
* Easy comparison between clubs
* Faster decision-making
* Clubs that match personal interests
* Greater confidence before joining
* A simple and convenient exploration process

---

## Value Map

### Products and Services

* UQ Club Discovery Hub MVP
* Club browsing interface
* Search functionality
* Category filtering
* Club detail views
* Join-club functionality
* User profile page

### Pain Relievers

* Organises club information into a consistent format
* Reduces effort required to compare clubs
* Helps students narrow options using filters
* Provides accessible club details in one location
* Reduces uncertainty during club selection

### Gain Creators

* Faster and more confident club decisions
* Better alignment between students and clubs
* Improved user experience when exploring opportunities
* Increased student engagement with clubs
* More informed participation decisions

---

## Competitor Insights

Existing solutions such as the UQ Union Clubs Portal, Market Day, social media, and word-of-mouth recommendations help students discover clubs but do not effectively support comparison and decision-making.

The MVP addresses this gap by combining searchable club information, filtering tools, and structured club profiles within a single platform.

---

## Evidence from MVP Testing

MVP testing showed that:

* Students appreciated having club information organised in one place.
* Search and filtering reduced the effort required to explore clubs.
* Club detail views helped users compare clubs more effectively.
* Participants reported greater confidence when evaluating club options.

These findings support the value proposition that structured information improves decision confidence.

---

## Conclusion

The key insight from Iterations 1–3 is that club discovery is not the primary problem.

Students already have multiple ways to discover clubs.

The real challenge is evaluating and choosing the right club.

The UQ Club Discovery Hub MVP addresses this challenge through structured club information, search functionality, category filtering, club detail views, and simple club engagement features that help students make more confident decisions.
