# Business Model Canvas (Iteration 3)

## 1. Customer Segments

### Primary Customers

* First-year UQ students
* Newly enrolled domestic and international students
* Students looking for clubs that match their interests, goals, and time commitments

### Secondary Customers

* Returning undergraduate students
* Postgraduate students
* UQ clubs and societies seeking suitable new members

---

## 2. Value Propositions

### For Students

* Reduce uncertainty when choosing clubs
* Provide structured and comparable club information
* Support confident decision-making through search, filtering, and detailed club profiles
* Save time when exploring club options

### For Clubs

* Increase visibility among relevant students
* Attract members who are a better fit
* Present club information in a consistent and accessible format

## 10. Accepted Hypotheses

### H1

Students experience difficulty choosing clubs because information is fragmented, unclear, or difficult to compare.

**Evidence:** Interviews across Iterations 1 and 2 consistently identified decision-making as a larger problem than discovery.

### H2

Structured club information improves decision confidence.

**Evidence:** MVP testing showed participants valued having club details organised in a consistent format.

### H3

Search and filtering reduce effort when exploring clubs.

**Evidence:** Users successfully used these features during MVP testing and reported that they improved usability.

### H4

Students prefer a single platform that combines club information in one place.

**Evidence:** Interview participants frequently mentioned needing to check multiple sources before making a decision.

---

## 11. Refuted Hypotheses

### H5

Students primarily need more clubs to choose from.

**Evidence:** Interviews showed that students already know many clubs exist; the challenge is understanding which club best fits them.

### H6

Club discovery alone solves the problem.

**Evidence:** Existing solutions already support discovery, but students still struggle to confidently choose a club.

---

## Key Insight

Students can already discover clubs through Market Day, social media, friends, and the UQ Union website.

The real problem is not discovery.

The real problem is decision confidence.

Therefore, the MVP focuses on helping students compare, evaluate, and choose clubs through structured information, search, filtering, and club detail views.
