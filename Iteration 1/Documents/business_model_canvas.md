# Business Model & Value Proposition

## Business Model Canvas

### Customer Segments
- UQ students who are interested in joining clubs  
- New students who are not familiar with what clubs are available  
- Students who want to try new activities but don’t know where to start  


### Value Propositions
- Help students find clubs that match their interests more easily  
- Provide clear and up-to-date information about clubs in one place  
- Make it easier to compare different clubs  
- Reduce uncertainty before joining a club  
- Make joining feel more comfortable, especially with friends  


### Channels
- A website or app where students can browse clubs  
- Social media (e.g. Instagram, Discord)  
- University platforms such as emails or student portals  


### Customer Relationships
- Mainly self-service, where students explore clubs on their own  
- Some social elements, such as seeing what friends are interested in  
- Building trust through clear and updated information  


### Revenue Streams
- Not a main focus at this stage  
- Possible future options:
  - Sponsored promotion for clubs  
  - Featured listings

## Hypothesis Validation

### Awareness
We believed that students are not aware of the full range of clubs at UQ.

- Result: 24 out of 25 students said they know about clubs, but most could only name a few  
- ✖ Refuted (partially)

This suggests that students are aware that clubs exist, but don’t have a strong understanding of specific clubs.


### Discovery
We believed that students find it difficult to discover clubs that match their interests.

- Result: 21 out of 25 students said they found it difficult  
- ✔ Accepted

Students described the process as scattered and unclear.  
However, some mentioned that it is much easier during Market Day, where everything is visible in one place.


### Social
We believed that students are more likely to join clubs if their friends are involved.

- Result: 22 out of 25 students mentioned friends as an important factor  
- ✔ Accepted

Many students said they feel more comfortable going with friends, and friends often help turn interest into action.


### Barriers
We believed that students face barriers that stop them from joining clubs.

- Result: 23 out of 25 students mentioned at least one barrier  
- ✔ Accepted

Common barriers include unclear information, not knowing what to expect, time concerns, and the effort required to find reliable details.

## Value Proposition Canvas (UQ Students)

### Customer Jobs
- Find clubs that match their interests  
- Understand what each club does  
- Decide whether it is worth joining  
- Join without feeling awkward  


### Pains
- Information is spread across different platforms  
- Hard to compare clubs  
- Not clear how to join  
- Unsure what the experience will be like  
- Worried about time commitment  


### Gains
- Easier way to discover clubs  
- Clear information before joining  
- More confidence in choosing a club  
- Feeling more comfortable when joining  


### Pain Relievers
- One place with updated and clear club information  
- Simple descriptions of activities and time commitment  
- Filters based on interests and availability  
- Showing social signals (e.g. friends or popularity)  


### Gain Creators
- Personalised recommendations  
- “What to expect” information for each club  
- Highlight beginner-friendly or popular clubs  
- Help students make decisions more quickly  


---

## Summary

From the interviews, the main issue is not awareness, but the difficulty of discovering and understanding clubs. Social influence and different barriers also affect whether students actually decide to join.
