From that we learnt that:

1. Students have basic awareness of UQ clubs in general, but lack in-depth understanding of specific club activities, values and details. The real issue is not "awareness of existence", but "awareness of specifics".

2. The lack of a centralized, accessible platform is the core pain point for students. Students struggle with fragmented information across multiple channels, and crave a one-stop solution that replicates the convenience of Market Day year-round.

3. Social connection is a critical driver of club participation. Students prioritize comfort and familiarity, and peer influence directly reduces the anxiety of joining a new group.

4. Uncertainty and friction in the decision-making process are the main barriers to participation. Students need transparent, upfront information to reduce risk and make confident decisions.
