# Team Values

As a team, we agreed on the following values while working on this project:

- **Communication**  
We keep each other updated on progress and discuss any issues early.

- **Responsibility**  
Each member takes ownership of their assigned tasks.

- **Collaboration**  
We support each other and share ideas when making decisions.

- **Respect**  
We respect different opinions and work together to reach agreement.

- **Consistency**  
We try to maintain a consistent style across our documents.
