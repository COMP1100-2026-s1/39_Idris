## Project Overview

This project explores how UQ students discover and join clubs, and aims to improve the overall experience.

From our interviews, we found that although most students are aware that clubs exist, many struggle to find, compare, and decide which clubs to join. As a result, students often lose interest before actually joining.


---

## Problem

Based on 25 interviews:

- 24/25 students were aware of clubs, but most could only name a few  
- 21/25 students found it difficult to discover suitable clubs  
- 23/25 students mentioned at least one barrier to joining  

Students described the process as scattered and unclear. Outside of Market Day, it is difficult to explore clubs efficiently.


---

## Target Users

- UQ students interested in joining clubs  
- New students unfamiliar with available clubs  
- Students looking to explore new interests  


---

## Key Insights

- Discovery is the main issue (accepted)  
- Social influence plays an important role (accepted)  
- Barriers reduce participation (accepted)  
- Awareness exists, but is limited (partially refuted)  


---

## Solution Idea

We propose a platform that:

- Centralises club information in one place  
- Makes it easier to compare different clubs  
- Provides clearer expectations before joining  
- Includes social features to reduce hesitation  


---

## Evidence

Our findings are based on 25 interviews.

- 21/25 students reported difficulty in discovering clubs  
- 22/25 students said friends influence their decisions  
- 23/25 students mentioned barriers  

Detailed transcripts can be found in the Interviews folder.
