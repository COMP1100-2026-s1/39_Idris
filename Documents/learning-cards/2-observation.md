We observed that:

1. 24 out of 25 students confirmed they know clubs exist, but the majority could only name a small number of specific clubs.

2. 21 out of 25 students reported significant difficulty finding suitable clubs, describing the current process as scattered, fragmented and unclear. Most students noted that Market Day is the only time the process becomes simple, due to centralized display.

3. 22 out of 25 students identified friends as a key factor in their club joining decisions. Many students stated they feel more comfortable attending club events with friends, and friends often turn passive interest into active action.

4. 23 out of 25 students mentioned at least one barrier to joining. The most common issues included unclear club information, uncertainty about the club experience, concerns about time commitment, and the effort required to find reliable details.
