Test Card 1 – Awareness
Hypothesis

We believe that students are not aware of the full range of clubs available at UQ.
Test

To verify this, we will interview students and ask them to list the clubs they know.
Metric

We will measure the number of clubs each student can name.
Success Criteria

We are right if more than 70% of students can name fewer than 5 clubs.

 Test Card 2 – Discovery Difficulty
Hypothesis

We believe that students find it difficult to discover clubs that match their interests.
Test

To verify this, we will ask students about their experience searching for clubs.
Metric

We will measure how many students report difficulty or confusion.
Success Criteria

We are right if at least 60% of students report difficulty.

Test Card 3 – Social Influence
Hypothesis

We believe that students are more likely to join clubs if their friends are involved.
Test

To verify this, we will ask students about the role of friends in their decision to join clubs.
Metric

We will measure how many students mention friends as a key factor.
Success Criteria

We are right if more than 50% of students mention social influence.
______________
 Test Card 4 – Participation Barriers
Hypothesis

We believe that students face barriers (such as time, effort, or uncertainty) that prevent them from joining clubs.
Test

To verify this, we will ask students why they have not joined clubs or what difficulties they faced.
Metric

We will measure how many students mention at least one barrier.
Success Criteria

We are right if more than 50% of students report at least one barrier.
