# Test Cards & Interview Guide (UQ Clubs Discovery Project)
---
## Test Card 1 – Awareness
**Hypothesis**
We believe that students are not aware of the full range of clubs available at UQ.
**Test**
To verify this, we will interview students and ask them to list the clubs they know.
**Metric**
We will measure the number of clubs each student can name.
**Success Criteria**
We are right if more than 70% of students can name fewer than 5 clubs.
---
## Test Card 2 – Discovery Difficulty
**Hypothesis**
We believe that students find it difficult to discover clubs that match their interests.
**Test**
To verify this, we will ask students about their experience searching for clubs.
**Metric**  
We will measure how many students report difficulty or confusion.
**Success Criteria**  
We are right if at least 60% of students report difficulty.
---
## Test Card 3 – Social Influence
**Hypothesis**  
We believe that students are more likely to join clubs if their friends are involved.
**Test**  
To verify this, we will ask students about the role of friends in their decision to join clubs.
**Metric**  
We will measure how many students mention friends as a key factor.
**Success Criteria**  
We are right if more than 50% of students mention social influence.
---
## Test Card 4 – Participation Barriers
**Hypothesis**  
We believe that students face barriers (such as time, effort, or uncertainty) that prevent them from joining clubs.
**Test**  
To verify this, we will ask students why they have not joined clubs or what difficulties they faced.
**Metric**  
We will measure how many students mention at least one barrier.
**Success Criteria**  
We are right if more than 50% of students report at least one barrier.
---
# Interview Questions
## Introduction
- What year are you in?
- What do you study?
- How long have you been at UQ?
---
## Section 1 – Awareness (Test Card 1)
- What clubs or extracurricular activities at UQ can you name right now?
**Follow-up:**
- How did you hear about those clubs?
- Which one do you know the most about? Why?
- Do you think there are many clubs you don’t know about? Why?
- Can you remember the first time you heard about a club? What happened?
---
## Section 2 – Discovery Experience (Test Card 2)
- Have you ever tried to find a club that matches your interests?
**If YES:**
- Can you walk me through exactly what you did?
- Where did you start?
- What were you expecting to find?
- Did the result match your expectations? Why or why not?
- Was it easy or difficult? Why?
- At what point did you feel confused or frustrated?
- Did you ever give up? What happened?
- If you had to do it again, what would you do differently?
**If NO:**
- Have you ever thought about joining a club?
- Why didn’t you take action?
- If you wanted to find a club, how would you start?
- Do you think it would be easy or difficult? Why?<
- What do you think would be the hardest part?
---
## Section 3 – Behaviour & Barriers (Test Card 4)
- Have you joined any clubs before?
**If YES:**
- What made you decide to join?
- How was the joining process?
- Was anything confusing or difficult?
- Did you ever consider quitting? Why?
**If NO:**
- Why haven’t you joined any clubs?
- What stopped you?
- Did you ever almost join but didn’t? What happened?
**Follow-up for everyone:**
- Was it more about time, effort, or uncertainty?
- Did you feel unsure which club to choose?<br/>> - How did you feel at that time?
---
## Section 4 – Social Influence (Test Card 3)
- Would you be more likely to join a club if your friends were also joining?
**Follow-up:**
- Why?
- Can you give an example of when your friends influenced your decision?
- Would you feel comfortable joining a club alone? Why or why not?
- How would your decision change if none of your friends joined?
---
## Section 5 – Needs & Expectations
- What would make it easier for you to discover and join clubs at UQ?
**Follow-up:**
- What kind of system or platform would help?
- What features would you expect?
- Would you like personalised recommendations? Why or why not?
- What would make you trust and use such a system?
---
## Closing
- Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience with clubs at UQ?
