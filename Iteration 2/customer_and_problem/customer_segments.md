# Customer Segments

## Primary Segment (Refined & Testable)
First-year and newly enrolled UQ students who:
- are interested in joining clubs
- actively explore campus activities
- feel uncertain when comparing club options
- lack clear expectations of what clubs are actually like

---

## Characteristics
- Limited prior experience with UQ clubs
- Rely on Market Day, social media, or friends for discovery
- Use interest and time as initial filters
- Often delay decisions due to uncertainty

---

## Key Behaviours (Evidence-based)
- Can find clubs, but struggle to choose one
- Seek information about:
  - activities
  - schedules
  - fees
  - club culture
- Prefer to “explore first” before committing

---

## Secondary Segment
Returning students and postgraduates who:
- have tighter schedules
- prioritise relevance and flexibility
- prefer efficient decision-making

---

## Early Adopters
Students who:
- are currently searching for clubs
- feel overwhelmed by unclear information
- want a faster and more confident way to decide

---

## Key Insight
Students are not lacking options —
