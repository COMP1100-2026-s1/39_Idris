# Problem Summary

## Core Problem
Students cannot confidently decide which club to join.

---

## Evidence from Interviews
- Most students can already discover clubs (Market Day, social media)
- However, they cannot clearly understand:
  - what the club actually does
  - what the experience is like
  - whether it fits their schedule

---

## Key Problems

### 1. Decision Uncertainty (Primary Issue)
- Club information is:
  - vague
  - incomplete
  - too promotional
- Users cannot visualise real experiences

Result:
Low confidence → delayed or no decision

---

### 2. Limited Effectiveness of Existing Tools
- Filters help narrow options but do not support decisions
- Social features increase curiosity but not commitment

---

### 3. Conditional Conversion
- Simple joining processes increase willingness
- But only when users already trust the club

---

## Root Cause
Lack of clear, structured, and trustworthy club information.

---

## Impact
- Students hesitate or avoid joining
- High interest does not translate into participation
- Clubs struggle to attract suitable members

---

## Key Insight
The main problem is not discovery —  
it is **decision-making confidence**.
