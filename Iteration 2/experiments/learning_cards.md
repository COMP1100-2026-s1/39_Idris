# Iteration Learning Card — UQ Club Discovery Platform

---

## Initial Hypothesis

Students struggle to discover suitable clubs efficiently.  
Providing filters, social features, clear information, and a simple joining process will improve both discovery and decision-making.

---

## What We Tested

We conducted 5 user interviews to test four key assumptions:

- H1: Filters improve club discovery and decision-making  
- H2: Social features increase willingness to join  
- H3: Clear information improves confidence in decisions  
- H4: A simple joining process increases participation  

---

## What We Learned

### 1. Discovery is Not the Main Problem
Users are generally able to find clubs through existing channels (e.g., Market Day, social media).  
The real challenge is deciding which club is suitable.

---

### 2. Decision-Making is the Core Problem
Users hesitate because:
- Information is vague or outdated  
- Activities are unclear  
- Time commitment is uncertain  

This creates low confidence and delays decisions.

---

### 3. Filters are Helpful but Limited
- Filters (interest, time) help narrow options  
- However, they do not solve uncertainty  

Users need additional filters:
- schedule  
- fees  
- location  
- activity type  
- club atmosphere  

---

### 4. Social Features Reduce Anxiety, Not Decide
- Seeing friends increases curiosity and comfort  
- Helps overcome first-time participation barriers  

However:
- Users will not join without personal interest  
- Social influence is supportive, not decisive  

---

### 5. Easy Joining Only Works After Trust is Built
- Users prefer fast, simple joining  
- But they will not join without sufficient information  

Some users prefer:
- saving or bookmarking  
- attending once before committing  

---

## Updated Insight

> The main problem is not discovering clubs, but making confident decisions due to unclear and insufficient information.

---

## Pivot / Iteration

### Before (Initial Focus)
- Improve discovery  
- Add filters  
- Add social features  
- Simplify joining  

---

### After (Refined Focus)
- Prioritise information clarity and transparency  
- Provide real, detailed, and trustworthy content  
- Support decision-making first, then discovery  

---

## Design Implications

- Standardised club profiles with:
  - clear schedules  
  - real activity examples  
  - time commitment  
  - fees and requirements  
  - club culture  

- Enhanced filtering system:
  - include practical filters (time, cost, location)  

- Soft engagement features:
  - save / follow  
  - attend before joining  

- Social features with privacy control  

- Simple joining process (after decision stage)  

---

## Conclusion

Improving information clarity and trust is the most critical factor.  
All other features (filters, social, joining) only become effective once users feel confident in their decision.
