# Test Cards – UQ Club Discovery Platform
## Hypothesis 1: Smart Discovery (Filters)
**HYPOTHESIS**:  
We believe that students can find suitable clubs faster when using filters (interest, time, beginner level).

**TEST**:  
Ask students to use filters to find a club they like.

**METRIC**:  
- Time to find a club  
- User satisfaction (1–5)

**CRITERIA**:  
- 80% of users find a club within 2 minutes  
- Average satisfaction ≥ 4  

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**:  
1. Was it easy to find a club?  
2. What part was confusing?  
3. What filters do you want to add?


## Hypothesis 2: Social-driven Features
**HYPOTHESIS**:  
We believe that showing friends’ activity will increase students’ willingness to join clubs.

**TEST**:  
Show users a feature like “your friends joined this club” and ask them to explore.

**METRIC**:  
- % of users interested in joining  
- Likelihood to join (1–5)

**CRITERIA**:  
- At least 60% of users show interest  
- Average likelihood ≥ 4  

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**:  
1. Does seeing your friends affect your decision?  
2. Would you join a club with your friends?  
3. What social feature do you like?


## Hypothesis 3: Clear Club Information
**HYPOTHESIS**:  
We believe that clear club profiles (activities, time, “what to expect”) help students decide faster.

**TEST**:  
Ask users to read a club profile and decide if they want to join.

**METRIC**:  
- Time to make decision  
- Confidence level (1–5)

**CRITERIA**:  
- Average decision time < 1.5 minutes  
- Average confidence ≥ 4  

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**:  
1. Was the information clear?  
2. What information is missing?  
3. What do you want to know before joining?


## Hypothesis 4: Easy Joining Process
**HYPOTHESIS**:  
We believe that a simple step-by-step joining process will reduce difficulty and increase completion.

**TEST**:  
Ask users to join a club using a guided process.

**METRIC**:  
- % of users who complete joining  
- Ease of use (1–5)

**CRITERIA**:  
- At least 70% complete the process  
- Average ease score ≥ 4  

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**:  
1. Was the process easy to follow?  
2. Where did you feel confused?  
3. How can we improve it?
