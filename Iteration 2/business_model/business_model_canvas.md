# Business Model Canvas

## 1. Customer Segments
- First-year and newly enrolled UQ students (primary)
- Returning / postgraduate students
- Club organisers (secondary)

---

## 2. Value Proposition (Pain-focused)

### For Students
- Reduce uncertainty when choosing clubs
- Help them understand what to expect before joining
- Enable confident and faster decision-making

### For Clubs
- Attract more suitable members
- Improve engagement and retention
- Communicate real club experience effectively

---

## 3. Customer Relationships
- Self-service platform
- Decision-support experience (not just discovery)
- Personalised but non-intrusive interaction

---

## 4. Channels
- Web-based platform
- Mobile-friendly access
- Potential integration with university systems

---

## 5. Key Activities
- Structuring and standardising club information
- Ensuring accuracy and clarity of content
- Designing decision-support features
- Supporting discovery through filters

---

## 6. Key Resources
- Platform (system + interface)
- Structured club data
- User behaviour insights
- Development team

---

## 7. Key Partners
- UQ clubs and societies
- Student organisations
- University platforms (future)

---

## 8. Revenue Streams (Future)
- Featured listings for clubs
- Sponsorship opportunities
- Partnerships with student organisations

---

## 9. Cost Structure
- Development and maintenance
- Hosting and infrastructure
- Data management
- User acquisition

---

## Key Insight (Critical)

Users:
- can already discover clubs  
- cannot confidently choose one  

Therefore:
The core value is **decision confidence through clarity**,
