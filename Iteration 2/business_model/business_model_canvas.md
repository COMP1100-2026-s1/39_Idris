# UQ Club Discovery Platform  
*A Business Model Canvas (BMC)*

---

## Overview
This project proposes a centralized platform to help University of Queensland (UQ) students discover and **confidently choose** student clubs.

Based on 5 user interviews, the key insight is:
> Students do not struggle to find clubs — they struggle to make confident decisions due to unclear information.

---

## Customer Segments
- UQ students (especially undergraduates with busy schedules)  
- New students seeking social integration  
- Students exploring new interests but unsure what suits them  
- Student clubs and societies (secondary users)

---

## Value Proposition
**Help students confidently choose the right club, not just find one.**

- Centralised platform replacing fragmented discovery (e.g., Market Day)  
- Clear, structured, and trustworthy club information  
- Smart filtering (interest, time, difficulty, and extended filters)  
- Social proof (friends’ participation reduces uncertainty)  
- Fast and frictionless joining process  

---

## Channels
- Mobile app (primary channel)  
- Web platform  
- UQ official channels (email, Blackboard)  
- Market Day QR code onboarding  
- Social media (Instagram, WeChat)

---

## Customer Relationships
- Self-service exploration (filters and browsing)  
- Personalised recommendations  
- Save, bookmark, and follow features (soft commitment)  
- Social interaction (visibility of friends’ activity)  
- Reviews and feedback from members  

---

## Revenue Streams
- Freemium model for clubs (basic listing free, premium exposure paid)  
- Sponsored club promotions or featured listings  
- University partnerships or funding support  
- Event promotion services  

---

## Key Resources
- Platform (mobile app and website)  
- Club database (accurate and up-to-date information)  
- User data (preferences and behaviour)  
- Development team  
- Partnerships with UQ and student clubs  

---

## Key Activities
- Platform development and maintenance  
- Collecting and updating club information  
- UX design focused on clarity and decision-making  
- Managing filtering and recommendation systems  
- Club onboarding and support  
- Marketing and user acquisition  

---

## Key Partnerships
- University of Queensland (UQ)  
- Student clubs and societies  
- UQ Union and student organisations  
- IT infrastructure providers  
- Payment service providers (if applicable)

---

## Cost Structure
- Development costs (app and website)  
- Maintenance and cloud hosting  
- Marketing and user acquisition  
- Data management and moderation  
- Customer support  

---

## Key Takeaway
The primary problem is not discovering clubs, but making confident decisions.  

Clear, detailed, and trustworthy club information is the most critical factor, while filters, social features, and an easy joining process act as supporting elements.
