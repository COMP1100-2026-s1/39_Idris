# Value Proposition Canvas

## Introduction

The Value Proposition Canvas was used to identify the needs, frustrations, and desired outcomes of UQ students when discovering and joining clubs. It also helped align the proposed UQ Club Discovery Hub with these user needs.

---

## Customer Profile

The target customer segment is UQ undergraduate and postgraduate students seeking extracurricular involvement.

### Customer Jobs
- Discover clubs and societies
- Compare available options
- Meet new people
- Build skills and networks
- Join clubs that fit schedules and interests

### Pains
- Difficult to confidently choose clubs
- Vague or outdated information
- Unclear time commitment
- Overwhelming number of options
- Fear of joining the wrong club
- Social anxiety joining alone

### Gains
- Clear and trustworthy information
- Easy comparison between clubs
- Clubs suited to interests
- Welcoming communities
- Simple joining process

---

## Value Map

### Products and Services
- UQ Club Discovery Hub
- Verified club profiles
- Smart filtering system
- Timetable integration
- Low-friction joining system

### Pain Relievers
- Clear schedules and expectations
- Transparent fees and requirements
- Better comparison tools
- Lower commitment pressure
- Reduced uncertainty

### Gain Creators
- Faster confident decisions
- Better club-user fit
- More student participation
- Improved university experience

---

## Competitor Insights

Existing options such as Market Day, the UQ Union portal, and social media assist awareness but do not effectively support confident decision-making. This creates an opportunity for a more transparent and personalised platform.

## Conclusion

The Value Proposition Canvas showed that the key student problem is not discovering clubs, but confidently deciding which club to join. The proposed solution addresses this through clearer information, practical filters, and simplified engagement pathways.
