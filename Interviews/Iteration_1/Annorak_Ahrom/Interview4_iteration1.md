I think procrastinating, going out with my friends — like seeing them all go out — and again having FOMO would influence my decisions on school choices. When I should have been prioritizing academics and my studies, I was going out with my friends a lot, creating more social connections instead of prioritizing my studies, which ended up, I guess, biting me later on.

Speaker 1:
You mentioned that there were people you didn't like or who had a bad reputation in those specific clubs, which made you reluctant to join. However, if there was a club without those types of people, but you didn't have any friends either, would you still join?

Speaker 2:
I think so. It's not that I struggle with making connections or making friends or anything, but it's always that first step that I find really difficult. It kind of depends on my mood. In all honesty, if I'm in a pretty social mood, then I'll approach people first. But there will be times where I find it very difficult to approach people. I think it mostly depends — I hate to say — but it kind of depends on the vibe I get from the club. If I get the vibe that they already know each other and are already pretty close, then I'm going to feel quite intimidated and reluctant to join, since I have this assumption that they already have a tight connection. And I guess it's those concerns of like, will I get along with them or not? So I think it depends on the first impression that I get of that club.
